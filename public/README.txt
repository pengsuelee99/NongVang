# Instructions for adding photos and music

1. **Photos**: Place your photos in `public/images/`:
   - `photo1.jpg`
   - `photo2.jpg`
   - `photo3.jpg`
   - `photo4.jpg`
   - `photo5.jpg`

2. **Music**: Place your background music file in `public/music/`:
   - `birthday.mp3`

3. **Content / Texts**: Customize texts and names in `src/config.ts`.
